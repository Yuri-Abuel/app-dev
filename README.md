## About the Series
**One Piece** is easily one of my favorite anime and manga series of all time, created by **Eiichiro Oda**. It tells the story of **Monkey D. Luffy**, a stubborn and optimistic young pirate who gains rubber-like abilities after eating a Devil Fruit. Alongside his crew, he sails across the dangerous waters of the Grand Line in search of the legendary treasure known as the **"One Piece"** to earn the title of **King of the Pirates**.
